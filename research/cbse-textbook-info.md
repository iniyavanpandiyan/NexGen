# CBSE Textbook Structure (Research Notes)

## Current CBSE Curriculum Structure

### Classes Covered:
- **Class 9-10**: Foundation stage (pre-board exams)
- **Class 11-12**: Senior secondary (board exams)

### Subjects by Class:

**Class 9:**
- Mathematics, Science (Physics/Chemistry/Biology), Social Science (History/Geography/Civics/Economics), English, Hindi, Sanskrit (optional)

**Class 10:**
- Same as Class 9 but with more depth + additional languages

**Class 11:**
- Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics/Biology (stream choice)
- English, Hindi, etc.

**Class 12:**
- Physics/Chemistry/Maths OR Biology (depending on stream)
- English, Hindi, etc.

### Textbook Sources:
- **NCERT** (National Council of Educational Research and Training) - Primary source
- Published annually by NCERT for each class/subject combination
- Available as PDFs on ncert.nic.in (though access may be restricted)
- Also available through government e-content portals

### Edition Years (Recent):
- 2023-24 academic year textbooks
- 2024-25 academic year textbooks (latest)

### Key Regulations:
- CBSE follows NCERT textbooks for all subjects
- Textbooks are updated annually with minor revisions
- Some subjects have "additional" books for competitive exam prep
